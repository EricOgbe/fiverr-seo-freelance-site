Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No. No ethical SEO provider can guarantee a particular ranking or timeline. Search results depend on many factors outside the control of a single service provider.

Will on-page SEO immediately increase my traffic?

Not necessarily. Improvements can make pages clearer and better aligned with search intent, but results depend on competition, site authority, technical health, content quality, and how search engines respond over time.

Does this include technical SEO or backlink building?

Do not assume so. Check the live Fiverr package for its exact scope. This page focuses on on-page SEO; technical fixes and off-page work should only be expected when explicitly included.

What do you need from me?

Usually, the website URL, the pages included in your chosen package, and any relevant business or audience context. Follow the requirements listed on the Fiverr order page.

How much does the service cost?

Pricing and package details can change. Use the Fiverr gig link for the current price, delivery time, and deliverables before placing an order.

Is the sample audit a real client case study?

No. The sample finding on this page is an illustrative demonstration of the format and reasoning, not a real client result or a claim about a specific website.
